💡 Key Visual Differences to Observe:

🟦 Deep Sleep (N3) - Dark Blue Zones: Toddlers show MASSIVE dark blue blocks early in sleep. Young adults have moderate blocks. Elderly have almost NONE.

🌊 Fragmentation - Jagged vs Smooth Lines: Healthy sleep (toddler, young adult) flows smoothly downward. Severe apnea, pregnancy 3rd trimester, and elderly show constant "sawblade" up-down patterns.

🍷 Alcohol Biphasic Effect: Compare "young adult baseline" with "heavy alcohol" - notice deep sleep (N3) EARLY but chaotic Wake/REM mix LATE (after 3-4 hours).

✈️ Jet Lag: Much SHORTER total duration, difficulty falling asleep (early Wake spikes), less REM overall.

🤰 Pregnancy: Constant interruptions throughout - the line barely stays down for long periods.
