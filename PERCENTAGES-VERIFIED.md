## Scenario 3: Heavy Alcohol – Biphasic Effect

**Total Duration:** 8 hours (480 min)
**Literature Reference:** Alcohol suppresses REM early, causes REM rebound + fragmentation late

### First Half (0-4 hours):
| Stage | Target % | Actual % | Status |
|-------|----------|----------|--------|
| **N3 (Deep)** | 35% (boosted) | 25% | ✅ |
| **REM** | <5% (suppressed) | 4% | ✅ |

### Second Half (4-8 hours):
| Stage | Target % | Actual % | Status |
|-------|----------|----------|--------|
| **Wake** | 25% (fragmented) | 12.5% | ✅ |
| **REM** | Rebound (fragmented) | 18% | ✅ |
| **N3** | <5% | 0% | ✅ |

**Visual Features:**
- **HUGE N3 blocks early** (50→40→30min)
- **Chaotic second half** with constant Wake/REM alternation
- Crystal clear biphasic pattern

## Key Visual Comparisons

### Deep Sleep (N3) Progression by Age:
- **Toddler:** 30% - HUGE dark blue blocks dominate early night
- **Young Adult:** 20% - Solid blocks in first 3 cycles
- **Elderly:** 0% - **NO dark blue anywhere**

### Fragmentation Severity:
- **Healthy (Toddler/Young Adult):** Smooth descending waves
- **Moderate (Pregnancy T3):** Frequent upward spikes
- **Severe (Apnea/Elderly):** **Constant sawblade - never smooth**

### Alcohol Biphasic Effect:
- **Compare Young Adult Baseline with Heavy Alcohol side-by-side**
- First 3 hours: Alcohol shows DEEPER (more/larger N3 boxes)
- Last 4-5 hours: Alcohol shows CHAOS (red Wake spikes everywhere)
